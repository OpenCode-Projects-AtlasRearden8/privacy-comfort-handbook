# Welcome — start here

*Reading time: about 6 minutes. Chapters take 6 to 10 minutes each.*

This is a handbook about everyday digital privacy, written the way a patient friend would explain it: plainly, step by step, and without making you feel bad about anything. You are not doing privacy "wrong." Nobody is. Most of us just pick up habits from app defaults and never get a calm, honest tour of what is going on.

This is that tour.

## A promise before we start

- No lectures. Nothing in here will tell you that you are "doing it wrong."
- No scare tactics. You will not be told the internet is out to get you.
- No jargon walls. Every technical term gets a plain-English definition, and the heavy ones live in the [glossary](09-glossary.md).
- No guilt. If you only change one thing after reading this, that is a genuine win.

Privacy is a dial, not a switch. You do not need to turn it to zero. You just need to know the dial exists, and decide where you want it.

## The three ideas that carry most of the weight

If you read nothing else from this handbook, these three habits cover a surprising amount of ground:

1. **Never paste your keys.** Passwords, 2FA codes, ID numbers, card numbers: those never go into chats, forms, or AI tools you don't fully trust. Chapter [What to never paste](02-what-never-to-paste.md) explains why in one friendly analogy.
2. **Use long passwords and turn on 2FA where it matters most.** Where "matters most" means anything with your money or your people in it: email, banking, phone. Chapter [Passwords and 2FA, simply](03-passwords-and-2fa.md) makes this genuinely easy.
3. **Pause before urgency.** Almost every scam on Earth runs on the same fuel: "act right now." Chapter [Phishing red flags](04-phishing-red-flags.md) gives you a seven-flag checklist that fits on a sticky note.

Everything else — app settings, browser settings, breach checks — is bonus points. Nice-to-haves, not homework.

## How the chapters flow (loosely)

You can read them in any order. Each stands on its own. If you want a suggested path:

- **Most people:** chapters 02, 03, and 04, then the checklist. That is roughly one evening and covers the highest-impact stuff.
- **Sharing devices or raising kids:** chapter 06 next.
- **Already fixed your passwords:** chapter 05 (browser and phone tidy-up) and then 07 (breach check).

## What this handbook will not ask of you

- It will not ask you to quit your favorite apps and websites.
- It will not charge you anything, or route you through "free trials."
- It will not tell you to buy a VPN on day one. A VPN is a fine tool for some things; it is not the magic privacy switch that ads claim. Chapter 05 covers when it actually helps.
